Keeping the Lights Alive

Efforts to preserve Hong Kong's neon heritage have grown as more signs disappear.

Preserving Neon at M+

M+, the museum of visual culture in the West Kowloon Cultural District, has collected historic neon signs and original design drawings since 2013.

Its exhibition Conserving Neon Culture, which opened in 2021, examines the craft and conservation of these objects.

Preservation allows visitors to experience a part of Hong Kong's visual history that has become increasingly difficult to encounter on the streets.
