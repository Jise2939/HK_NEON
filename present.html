The Disappearing Glow

Once a defining feature of Hong Kong's streets, traditional neon signs are gradually disappearing. As the city modernizes, many familiar signs have been removed or replaced by LED displays.

Why Are the Signs Disappearing?

Safety Concerns

Large hanging signs can become dangerous as their structures age.

Maintenance Costs

Traditional neon signs require specialized skills and regular repairs.

Modern Technology

LED signs are often cheaper to maintain and more energy efficient.
